THE SCIENCE, SIMPLIFIED

What are bispecific antibodies?

Think of them as molecular connectors designed to recognize two different targets instead of just one.

✧

One antibody, two targets

A bispecific antibody (BsAb) is engineered to bind two different antigens. Depending on the design, it may bring two cells close together or block two biological signals.

Target A＋Target B→BsAb
⌘

Why pair targets?

Combining targets may create complementary or synergistic effects. Researchers also need to consider where targets are expressed and possible safety concerns.

⌁

Where AI can help

Machine learning can compare candidate pairs using biological features, rank possibilities and highlight factors that may influence a prediction.

i

Important: A promising computational score is a research clue, not proof that a drug will be safe or effective. Laboratory and clinical validation are essential.

Antibody

Bispecific antibody
